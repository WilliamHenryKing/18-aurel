# AUREL design direction

## The feeling

Nocturnal, intimate, precise. AUREL is a place to slow down and notice the relationship between light, a surface and the way a room feels. Its audience is a visitor considering architecture, interiors or finishing, not a software buyer. The fictional nature of the work stays clear without overwhelming the experience.

## Visual grammar

Ink and charcoal form the page: `#141614`, `#222621`. Warm ivory `#e9e6de` carries the typography and aged brass `#c4ae83` provides a controlled accent. One pale olive/ivory invitation section changes the rhythm near the end. Images use generous scale; deliberately uneven project proportions resist a repeated card grid on the home page.

Cormorant Garamond supplies very large, delicate editorial headings with genuine italic cuts. Local Manrope is used for quiet metadata, navigation and body text. Type is part of the architecture of the composition: the hero's second line is indented, the invitation spans most of the page, and interior pages carry substantial opening statements.

The original generated residence at dusk is the opening world. The original travertine stair hall extends that world. The finishing image focuses on actual junctions and material scale. No invented client names, awards, testimonials, certification or completed-building claims are permitted. The four projects are openly described as speculative studies; the remaining bedroom photograph is a licensed atmosphere reference, never claimed as AUREL's design.

## Motion and spatial interaction

GSAP `useGSAP` scopes and reverts title entrances, editorial reveals and hero parallax. Native scrolling remains intact. Reduced-motion preference disables choreography initially; a visible footer control lets the visitor stop motion. No cursor replacement, scroll hijack, perpetual WebGL animation or loading screen is used.

The interactive light pavilion is a composed architectural section: floor, stepped plinth, perimeter stone walls, split roof and rooflight, fine timber screen, restrained bronze structure, built-in bench, daybed, small vessel and reflecting basin. Visitors alter hour, finish and view using native buttons. Daylight reveals the material surfaces; dusk warms the interior and deepens the shell. The frame is composed to retain the whole architectural object at phone widths.

## Responsive composition

Desktop uses wide margins and long views; tablet collapses the supporting editorial columns; phone turns the project sequence into an intentionally offset image journal. The header changes to an expandable navigation below 540px. All real controls remain available at 320px; the pavilion controls stack at the narrowest width. The enquiry page never requires personal information.

## Acceptance boundary

Source/type/lint checks establish implementation health. Actual browser captures, interaction, keyboard, reduced-motion, image loading, WebGL fallback, console/network and responsive checks establish local experience evidence. They do not establish real clients, completed architecture, user acceptance or public deployment. Root owns the shared visual/browser review and authorised release.
